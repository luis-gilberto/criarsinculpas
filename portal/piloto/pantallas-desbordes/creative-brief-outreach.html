Art direction

Do
  • Cream / blush / navy / peach
  • Cormorant for the hero line
  • One idea per frame
  • Real calm, human warmth
Don’t
  • Purple gradients, glow, emoji piles
  • “NEW / LAUNCH / JOIN NOW”
  • Clinical or therapy language
  • Pricing or membership CTAs

Success read (directional)

With only 10–15 participants, inspect the funnel — do not treat percentages as hard pass/fail:

  • Invited → started → identified → situation → orientation → path complete → plan saved → feedback submitted
  • Read comments in Formspree (pilot_01_feedback)
  • Manual follow-up: did they try something from the plan during a later screen transition?
