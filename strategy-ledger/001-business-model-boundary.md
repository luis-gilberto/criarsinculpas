# Strategic Decision Record 001: The CSC Business Model Boundary
**Date:** June 8, 2026
**Status:** Locked & Executing
**Context:** V2 Site Retrofit & Curriculum Architecture

## 1. The Core Breakthrough
The business model is no longer a guess; it is a structural boundary between **First Aid** and **Rehab**.
*   **Free = Understanding & Emergency Response (First Aid).** We give away the tools to stop the bleeding tonight.
*   **Paid = Application & Transformation (Rehab).** We sell the guided, step-by-step physical therapy to rewire the family dynamic so they stop bleeding in the first place.

## 2. The Ecosystem Map
Every asset now has a defined, measurable role in the funnel:
1.  **Instagram (The Signal):** Where the community breathes. Generates emotional demand.
2.  **Web / Protocols (The First Aid):** Generates massive trust. The parent arrives in chaos, uses the free tool, and experiences immediate relief. The brand becomes their hero.
3.  **The Método Page (The X-Ray):** The diagnostic tool. It teaches the *what* and the *why* (The 3 Chapters, the 5-step cycle). It creates comprehension, which produces a felt need for guided execution.
4.  **The 8 Modules (The Rehab):** The core paid product. The transformation. The "how to do this with my specific child."

## 3. The Visual Doctrine (V2)
The UI was retrofitted to support this exact psychology:
*   **Colors:** Free protocols use specific emotional color-coding (Peach = Emergency, Slate = Restoration). The Method uses structural brand colors (Navy, Cream).
*   **Imagery:** "Object in an empty room" stock photos were killed. The system is represented by **Monoline Illustration** (showing the mechanics of the method), while the emotional trust is carried by **Real Human Photography** (Hero and Nari's portrait).
*   **The Vibe:** Editorial Calm. Primer-level structure with Caracas warmth.
